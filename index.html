The problem

Physical queues waste everyone's time

Crowds, uncertainty and long waits hurt visitors and organisers alike. Smart Queue turns a crowd into a schedule.

Without Smart Queue

  • Hundreds of people arrive at once and wait for hours
  • Nobody knows when their turn will actually come
  • Overcrowded venues, tension, and lost productivity
  • No-shows leave gaps nobody can fill

With Smart Queue

  • Everyone gets an assigned time and queue number
  • Live countdown and position, right on your phone
  • Calm venues with only the right people present
  • Absent slots are released and reassigned instantly
How it works

From sign-up to served in three steps

Register once

Create your account with your name, email and phone, then verify with a one-time code.

Get your slot

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. Smart Queue installs straight from your browser. Open the link, add it to your home screen, and it behaves like a regular app.

How do I get a time slot?

Register in the app. The organiser of the event then finds you by name or phone number and assigns you a slot. You're notified right away.

When will I be reminded about my turn?

Smart Queue alerts you 10 minutes before your slot. You also get notified whenever your slot is assigned, changed or cancelled.

What if I can't make my slot?

If the organiser has enabled it, you can request a reschedule from the app and the admin approves or rejects it. Absent slots are released so someone else can use them.

Who can create queues?

Admin accounts, which are created by a Super Admin. Regular users register themselves and receive slots; Super Admins are set up by developers only.

Is this a finished production product?

The current app is a working demo that stores data on your device. A production version with cloud accounts and push notifications while the app is closed is the next step.
